Executive Summary

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY · VENUES AND ASSET CLASSES

This report is published by RWA Foundation using data from DefiLlama's RWA perpetuals dataset. It covers the onchain market for perpetual futures referencing real-world assets: equities, equity indices, precious metals, energy, foreign-exchange pairs, ETFs, private equity, bonds, and AI and compute infrastructure. All figures are 30-day trailing figures as of the report date unless otherwise noted.

The scope of this report is venues and reference assets, not chains. It ranks the top venues by 30-day volume and open interest, breaks volume down by DefiLlama's asset-class taxonomy and by the underlying reference-asset group, and ranks the individual markets generating the most trading activity. No protocol-level infrastructure metrics beyond fees are included, this is a market-structure view of the venues and the assets referenced on them.

Across DefiLlama's tracked RWA perp dataset, 835 active markets across 16 venues generated $128.4B in 30-day volume, with $4.86B in aggregate open interest and an estimated $36.9M in protocol fees. Trade.xyz, a Hyperliquid HIP-3 deployer settling on Hyperliquid's core matching engine, dominates with roughly 70% of all RWA perp volume, more than the next fifteen venues combined.

Stock-perps are the largest asset class at $66.48B (51.8% share) across 580 markets, followed by commodity-perps at $30.37B (23.7%) and broad equity-index perps at $14.97B (11.7%). By reference-asset group, public equities account for over half of volume, with precious metals and equity indices each carrying a roughly 13-14% share, and oil close behind at 9.3%.

05 — THE MARKET IN SUM

Closing Statement

The RWA perpetuals market, taken as a whole, is new and sharply concentrated. A single Hyperliquid-settled venue, Trade.xyz, carries roughly seven in every ten dollars of volume and three-quarters of open interest, and within that venue a small cluster of tickers does an outsized share of the work: two broad equity indices, a run of semiconductor and memory names, and a handful of precious-metals and energy contracts.

"Memory and semiconductor exposure, not equities broadly, is what is actually driving single-market volume on the dominant venue."

Asset-class figures suggest a market roughly evenly split between equities, commodities, and indices, but the top-15-markets view tells a narrower story: memory and semiconductor exposure, not equities broadly, is what is actually driving single-market volume on the dominant venue. Precious metals and oil, tracked separately as commodity-perp and by reference-asset group, together account for close to a quarter of the market and remain concentrated around a handful of gold, silver, and crude contracts split across Trade.xyz and variational.

The long tail below the top eight venues, entropy through paradex, together carries well under 2% of volume despite offering meaningful market count and, in Ostium's case, open interest disproportionate to volume - a pattern consistent with a market still early in venue consolidation, where liquidity has not yet spread evenly across the growing number of places to trade these products.
