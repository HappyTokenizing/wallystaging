Tokenized Stocks WeeklySeptember 28, 2026

Weekly tokenized stock market cap, holders and transfer activity.

PUBLISHEDSeptember 28, 2026
DATA WINDOWSee the original report below
PERIODPast 7 days; published September 28
Original article on X ↗
Token Terminal · ORIGINAL RESEARCH8 FIGURES

@Securitize’s $SECZ dominated stock growth over the past 7 days, adding $132.0M in market cap, nearly 10x the next asset. Behind it, SpaceX exposure stayed in demand as @binance's SPCXb added $13.3M and @xStocksFi's SPCXx $3.6M. Strategy was the other standout, with the Binance (MSTRb, +$12.1M) and xStocks (MSTRx, +$10.5M) versions together adding $22.6M. The rest of the top 10 were mostly single-digit millions, including INTCb, QQQb and AMDb. Tokenized stocks now total $3.6B across 4,555 assets. At issuer level, the SECZ surge put @Securitize well ahead, it added $131.1M in tokenized stock market cap over the past 7 days, more than 7x the rest of the top 8 combined. @xStocksFi was a distant second at $17.5M, helped by its MSTRx and SPCXx listings. After that, growth was spread thinly: @superstateInc (+$5.2M), @coinbase (+$3.5M), @binance (+$1.8M), @Ondo (+$1.7M) and @DinariGlobal (+$1.6M) all added under $6M. Across 23 issuers, tokenized stocks now total $3.6B.

The SECZ surge also decided which chain led, @avax added $131.2M in tokenized stock market cap over the past 7 days, which lines up almost exactly with Securitize's growth. @solana was a distant second at $16.0M, followed by @XLayerOfficial at $8.5M, while @base, @BNBCHAIN and @arbitrum each added between $1M and $4M. @ethereum was the only major chain to shrink, down $5.6M, as tokenized stock activity kept moving to faster, cheaper networks. Tokenized stocks now run across 17 chains.

@xStocksFi's NVDAx drew the most new holders over the past 7 days, adding 18.0K. METAx followed with 13.7K, then HIMS with 13.3K. Retail favourites were well represented further down: DJT (+10.4K), SPCXx (+10.3K) and GMEx (+8.7K) all made the top six, and @RobinhoodApp's NVDA and MSTR tokens each added 5K to 6K holders. This is said to be linked to the recent memefi trend where traders will buy a memecoin and then be distibuted a tokenized stock. $SECZ, which dominated market cap growth, didn't make the list at all, suggesting its gains came from a small number of large holders rather than broad retail demand. Tokenized stocks now have 4.5M holders across 5,247 assets.

@RobinhoodApp added the most new tokenized stock holders over the past 7 days, 83.2K, narrowly ahead of @xStocksFi at 73.4K, much of it from NVDAx and METAx. @Backpack was third with 44.1K. Those three issuers accounted for about 90% of the top five's new holders, well ahead of @binance (+14.4K) and @Ondo (+8.9K). @Securitize led market cap growth by a wide margin but didn't make the top five for holders, a sign of how differently institutional and retail flows are showing up onchain.

On the holder side, @solana led by a wide margin, adding 123.5K new tokenized stock holders over the past 7 days, mostly from xStocks and Backpack users. Robinhood Chain was second with 83.2K, matching Robinhood's own issuer total, so all of its growth is happening on its own chain. @BNBCHAIN added 23.2K, followed by @base (+8.3K) and @XLayerOfficial (+2.9K). Avalanche led market cap growth but didn't make the holder top five, which points to institutional capital driving most of that move.

@RobinhoodApp's DJT led, with transfer volume up $806.1M over the past 7 days, followed by Binance's METAc at $596.0M. Meta showed up twice in the top 10: METAc and @xStocksFi's METAx together added over $670M in volume. iShares' FXIon (+$161.6M), AAPLc (+$122.8M) and NVDAx (+$106.8M) completed the top five. Newer thematic tokens PHOTON and NEOCLOUD also made the list, each adding around $80M. In total, $20.4B in tokenized stocks changed hands over the week.

By issuer, @coinbase led tokenized stock transfer volume growth over the past 7 days, adding $350.5M. @RobinhoodApp came second with $210.5M, driven largely by its DJT token. Reserve followed with $171.6M and @xStocksFi with $144.1M, and @Ondo completed the top five at $85.2M. Across 20 issuers, $20.4B in tokenized stocks changed hands during the week.
